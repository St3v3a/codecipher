Privacy Policy

This page explains what personal data codecipher.ai collects, why, who else handles it, and the rights you have over it. Last updated 18 October 2026.

Who is responsible

The data controller is CodeCipher Ltd, Sotira, Famagusta, Cyprus.

What we collect

This site has no forms, accounts, analytics, advertising cookies or other tracking, and collects no personal data itself. Fonts are served from this site, so no font provider sees your visit.

If we add a contact form, this page will be updated to name the provider and what it receives.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, object to its processing, or complain to a supervisory authority. In Cyprus that is the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection. To exercise any of these rights, write to CodeCipher Ltd at the address above.
