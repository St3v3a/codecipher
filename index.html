// service: post-quantum

Post-quantum secure networking & messaging

Encrypted traffic intercepted today can be stored and decrypted the day a cryptographically relevant quantum computer arrives. If your data must stay secret for a decade, the migration clock is already running.

01 — Today. Your traffic crosses the network sealed with classical key exchange (RSA, ECDH). It looks safe.

02 — Harvest now. An adversary doesn't need to break it. They record the ciphertext and wait.

03 — Decrypt later. A cryptographically relevant quantum computer runs Shor's algorithm. The classical handshake — and everything behind it — opens.

04 — Post-quantum. Lattice-based ML-KEM key encapsulation replaces the vulnerable handshake. Recorded or not, the channel stays sealed.
